# P136 V0.008 — Catchment-area readiness / 集水面積資料準備

Status: **DATA AUDIT REQUIRED — NO HYDROLOGICAL SIMULATION YET**.

## Purpose
Support a uniform-rainfall experiment (e.g. 10 mm over an entire verified basin, runoff coefficient 100%) without treating river length as land area or presenting uncalibrated discharge as flash-flood predictions.

## Current verified inputs
- Published preliminary HydroRIVERS network: 443 reach features with `id`, `downstream_id`, and geometry.
- Precomputed upstream lists and reach lengths: `data/published/tamsui-network-index.json`.
- Basin boundary **not verified**; the 443 reaches were selected by an approximate mouth and a northern Taiwan crop.
- No catchment polygons or independently validated local/upstream contributing area are currently published.

## Required next source audit
1. Inspect the actual HydroRIVERS Asia DBF fields, including `UPLAND_SKM` and `DIST_DN_KM` **if present**; record exact field names, meanings, units, missing values, and product documentation. `UPLAND_SKM` is a candidate cumulative upstream drainage-area estimate, **not** a local incremental catchment polygon or proof of basin completeness.
2. Obtain an independently documented basin polygon or HydroBASINS subset, review usage/redistribution rights, coordinate reference system, source version and spatial scale.
3. Compare river outlet and tributary alignment with the independent boundary. Investigate missing reaches near the crop envelope and cross-divide discrepancies. Do not assert that northeastern Taiwan or any mountain region drains to Tamsui without a verified divide.
4. Match each river reach to a catchment polygon using source-defined identifiers or a documented reproducible spatial workflow. Reject many-to-many or unmatched cases rather than guessing.
5. Derive and validate local catchment areas, and verify that cumulative upstream areas obey the downstream topology. Report discrepancies and precision limitations.

## Mathematical contract for later rainfall prototype
For a *verified* area A in km², uniform rainfall depth P in mm, and runoff coefficient C:

`V_m3 = P_mm * A_km2 * 1000 * C`

This estimates event runoff **volume** only. Peak discharge (m³/s), hydrograph timing, flood stage and inundation require additional rainfall duration/distribution, travel-time/routing parameters, storage, channel capacity and calibration. Do not call volume a flash-flood prediction.
